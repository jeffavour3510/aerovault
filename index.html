Questions before you book

Do I need a pilot license to rent?

No — for consumer-class drones under local weight thresholds, no license is required. We'll walk you through the airspace rules for your shoot location at pickup. Commercial jobs near restricted zones may need clearance, which we can help arrange.

What happens if the drone is damaged?

Minor wear from normal use isn't charged. Crash or water damage is assessed against your deposit first; anything beyond that is billed at repair cost, capped and disclosed before you sign.

Can I extend a rental mid-shoot?

Yes, if the unit isn't booked next — message ground support and we'll extend and adjust your invoice.

Is training required before renting the FPV combo?

We recommend at least Track 01 before flying FPV, since manual acro control has a steeper learning curve than GPS-stabilized drones. Ask us and we'll advise based on your experience.
